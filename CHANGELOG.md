## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-19

### Added

- CommonMark AST calculation with Unicode-aware word counting and structured results.
- Hyde page integration, configurable page types and reading speed, and the opt-in `@readingTime` Blade directive.
- Unit tests, real Hyde build integration coverage, Composer checks, static analysis, formatting checks, and CI.
